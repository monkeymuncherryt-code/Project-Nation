PROJECT NATION V5.0 — WORLD & LEGACY
===================================

Launch (Windows or macOS): extract the ZIP, then double-click index.html inside the Project_Nation_V5_World_Empire folder. Keep all JS and CSS files next to index.html. Runs without external services or installation.

MAIN NEW FEATURES
- Instant wars. Select a country on the Earth map, click Instant War, confirm. Win or lose immediately. A victory gains 12–28 percentage points of the selected mapped country; a defeat loses 8–20 points from your own mapped territory. The outcome changes map shading and is saved. The chance is based on simplified fictional ratings, NOT real-world military capability. No tactical combat is simulated.
- A purple overlay shows partial region ownership. Land fractions are represented as a percentage of each approximate country shape; these are not surveyed province borders.
- Succession & generations. Open Leader > Appoint a successor/retire. The new leader inherits the full nation, money, cities, territories, history, diplomatic relationships and sports progress. Elections can require succession after a term limit or loss. Former leaders are archived in the V5 Hub history.
- V5 Hub. Citizen group satisfaction; buy/sell national resources (food, energy, materials, technology); airline/rail routes between your cities; six research projects; found an international organization and invite friendly countries; social news, and historical timeline.
- Form alliances in diplomacy. Allied countries are protected from declaring war until you end the alliance.
- Football league fixture schedule, seasons, champions, and club training.
- More original country-decision events and follow-up events.
- V4's detailed Flag Studio, government, city builder, Shop, and founding modes are retained.
- V4 Calm Pace stays: 0.5x = 2 minutes/game month, 1x = 1 minute, 3x = 20 seconds. Manual advance remains.

SAVES
- This build uses a new V5 browser save slot and attempts to load older V4 / V3 saves.
- ALWAYS use Export to back up a treasured nation before an upgrade or moving browsers/devices.
- Local browser storage often differs per folder or device. If Resume does not show your older save, import the JSON file you previously exported.
- For iPhone Safari, host the folder from an HTTPS site; opening local HTML via Files may not reliably save progress.

LIMITATIONS / WHAT ISN'T IMPLEMENTED
- War and diplomacy are fictional abstract strategy choices. There are no battle maps, tactical combat or real-world military modeling.
- Land ownership is approximate region fraction shading, not detailed provincial border redrawing.
- The economy, population, elections and social messages are simplified procedural game systems, not factual current-country statistics.
- Sports uses simulated fixtures, not interactive football play. No online multiplayer or live external news.
- Research, international organizations, market prices and citizen cohorts are small playable systems; not all long-term V5 roadmap features (full transfer market, nationwide elections with parties, decades-long macroeconomics) are complete.

SUPPORT: Open the browser developer console (F12) if there is a problem, and share the error text to help fix it.
